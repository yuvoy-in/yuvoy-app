There is one airport, and everything else is a boat. That single fact shapes
every Andaman itinerary more than anything you choose.

## By air, which is how nearly everyone comes

Every flight lands at **Veer Savarkar International Airport in Port Blair
(IXZ)**. There is no other airport you can fly into, and there is no airport on
Swaraj Dweep or Shaheed Dweep — the islands most people are actually going to.

Direct flights run from **Chennai, Kolkata, Delhi, Bengaluru and Hyderabad**.
Chennai is the shortest hop at around two hours and is the most common way in.

**Book the earliest arrival you can bear.** Not for the day itself, but for the
morning after: inter-island ferries leave early, and a late-afternoon landing
means your first island morning is already gone.

## By sea, which is a different holiday

Passenger ships sail to Port Blair from **Chennai, Kolkata and
Visakhapatnam**, roughly three to four times a month.

They are slow in a way that is easy to underestimate:

- **Chennai** — around 60 hours
- **Kolkata** — around 66 to 70 hours, the longest of the three

That is two and a half to three days at sea, each way. Tickets come from the
shipping office at the relevant port rather than an app, and are worth buying
well in advance.

**This is a decision, not a saving.** If the crossing is the point — and for
some people it genuinely is — it is a remarkable way to arrive. If you are
trying to save money on a week's holiday, six days of it are now boat.

Sailing dates move. Confirm with the shipping office rather than a blog,
including this one.

## The connection that catches people

The trip is almost always: **fly to Port Blair → ferry to an island → ferry
back → fly home.** Four moves, and two of them are at the mercy of the sea.

- **Do not book a same-day flight-to-ferry connection.** If the flight is late
  you miss the boat, and the next one may be tomorrow.
- **Do not fly out the same morning you leave an island.** This is the one that
  actually ruins trips. A cancelled or delayed ferry on your last morning and
  you are watching your flight leave from the wrong island.
- **Sleep in Port Blair the night before you fly home.** One night, and it
  removes the only genuinely unrecoverable failure in the itinerary.

## Between the islands

Ferries run Port Blair → Swaraj Dweep → Shaheed Dweep and back, several times
a day in season, operated by both government and private services. Journey
times and which islands connect directly vary by operator and by season — the
detail is in [which island should you stay
on](/guides/which-andaman-island).

The thing worth internalising: **in rough weather the ferries stop.** Not
"run late" — stop. That is the mechanism by which weather changes an Andaman
itinerary, and it is why every plan here should survive losing one morning.
